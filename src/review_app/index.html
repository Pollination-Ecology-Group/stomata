🌿 Reviewer Guide & Digital Calipers

How to inspect and verify stomata measurements to benchmark precision for ploidy sizing.

1. Anatomical Measurement Dimensions

📏 Major Axis (Full Stoma Length)

Spans between the two guard cell junctions (poles). Key trait for ploidy level discrimination (diploids 2x vs tetraploids 4x).

📏 Minor Axis (Full Stoma Width)

Cross-sectional diameter perpendicular to the major axis at the centroid. Includes both guard cells and the pore.

🔵 Central Pore Aperture Width

Inner central opening diameter measured along the minor axis. Critical proxy for stomatal conductance ($g_{\text{smax}}$).

🟢 Guard Cell Width

Radial thickness of one guard cell: $\frac{L_{\text{minor}} - P_{\text{width}}}{2}$. Turgor regulation trait.
